# 🚀 TitanPulse: AI-Driven Semiconductor Financial Intelligence & Competitive Analytics

> *A high-performance analytics and visualization suite engineered during my professional training at **ITG** to benchmark financial health, revenue distribution, and profit margins across semiconductor giants: NVIDIA, Intel, and TSMC.*

---

## 🔥 Overview

In the hyper-competitive semiconductor landscape, raw financial data tells only half the story. Developed as part of my advanced technical training at **ITG**, **TitanPulse** bridges the gap between raw ledger inputs and executive-level strategic insights. Powered by **Python**, **Pandas**, and **Gradio**, this system features a dual-tab architecture delivering instant financial calculations, margin breakdowns, and dynamic visual dashboards.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🏢 ITG Internship Milestone:** Designed and implemented as a capstone project showcasing enterprise-grade software development and financial analytics.
* **⚡ Real-Time Financial Calculator & Comparator:** Automatically computes net profit and profit margins for NVIDIA, Intel, and TSMC based on custom revenue and expense inputs.
* **📊 Visual Performance Dashboard:** Renders interactive, multi-metric bar charts (`gr.BarPlot`) comparing revenue and profit structures across all three industry heavyweights.
* **🧹 Interactive State Management:** Built-in `Generate` and `Clear` automation handlers for instant data repopulation and workspace resets.
* **🌐 Production-Ready UI:** Deployed seamlessly via Gradio with cloud-sharing capabilities for rapid stakeholder demonstrations.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

* **Core Logic:** Python 3.x, NumPy, Pandas
* **Interface & UI:** Gradio (`gr.Blocks`, `gr.Tabs`, `gr.BarPlot`)
* **Environment & Context:** Developed under the professional mentorship and training framework at **ITG**
